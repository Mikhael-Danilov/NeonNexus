## Full description

Neon Nexus is a neon space shooter where you protect your Mothership from
endless enemy waves.

Move, shoot, keep the Mothership alive. Waves grow stronger, the arena grows
with you, and chain kills multiply your score.

**Weapons** — unlocked by total kills:

- Pulse — the starter gun, a dependable auto-cannon
- Laser — continuous beam, 25 kills
- Spread — multi-shot barrage, 75 kills
- Missiles — homing, 150 kills
- Warp blink — instant teleport on slot 5 with a cooldown

**Enemies** — seven behavior types: chasers, zigzags, shooters, tanks,
splitters, orbiters and bombers. Each calls for its own tactic.

**Powerups**: shield, score multiplier and weapon overdrive drop from enemies.

**The Mothership** takes hits and fights back with turrets — clear the space
around her.

**Controls** — finger or mouse: tap/drag moves the ship and fires in AUTO
mode; the MODE button toggles AUTO / MOVE / SHOOT; pinch zooms the camera;
slots 1–4 switch weapons, slot 5 is the warp blink.

**Saves**: the high-score table lives in your browser, progress survives
between sessions.

The game runs right on the page — no install needed. Works in current
Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (WebGL required).
